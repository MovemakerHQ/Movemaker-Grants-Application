2. *Project Overview:*  
Aptos AgroConnect is a digital platform connecting farmers directly with buyers using Aptos blockchain, removing unnecessary middlemen and boosting farmers' profits.

3. *Team Member and Background:*  
My name is Abubakar Musa, a software developer and agribusiness specialist from Nigeria. I have experience building blockchain apps and collaborating with local farmer associations.

4. *Project Goal and Benefits:*  
The goal is to help farmers access buyers easily, receive payments quickly, and do transparent business. This will promote Aptos adoption in Africa and revolutionize agricultural trade.

5. *Project Name & Short Description*  
‎_Aptos Quest_ is an educational mobile game for Africans that teaches the basics of blockchain and rewards users with Aptos tokens as they complete learning challenges.
‎
‎6. *Project Objectives*  
‎- To onboard millions of new users into the Aptos ecosystem by using local languages and relatable content.  
‎- To make learning blockchain easy, interactive, and fun, especially for young people in Africa.  
‎- To create a sustainable platform that continuously engages, educates, and rewards its users.
‎
‎7. *How the Project Works*  
‎- Players create avatars and take part in short lessons and quizzes about blockchain.  
‎- Game levels include puzzle-solving, real-world examples, and community challenges.  
‎- Users earn Aptos tokens and rare NFTs as they complete each stage.  
‎- A leaderboard encourages healthy competition and shows top learners.
